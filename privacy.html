Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy covers ReadMe and the other apps made by Simple Tools.

What we collect

Nothing. Our apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

How the apps work

Any text you paste, type, or select is processed entirely on your device using its built-in text-to-speech engine. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to any server. The app does not request internet access.

Permissions

ReadMe reads your clipboard only when you tap “Paste Clipboard,” and only to place that text into the app so it can be read aloud. Clipboard contents are never stored or transmitted.

Third parties

Our apps contain no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Children

Our apps are suitable for all ages and collect no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email jyltech.usa.com.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
